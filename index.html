The method behind the marks

A strong business answer follows a structure that hits all four assessment objectives every time, and I teach two simple frameworks that make that structure easy to remember, which both of my guides are built around.

Once the structure feels automatic, you can spend your time in the exam thinking about the case study itself instead of worrying about how to lay out your answer.

PECAn PIE
A paragraph structure made up of Point, Explain, Contextualised Analysis and then Point Included Evaluation, where each part earns marks in a different assessment objective.
AJIM
A conclusion structure for the longer questions: Answer, Justify, It depends on, and Most important reason, which lets you pick a side and still show the examiner that you've weighed up the other points.
